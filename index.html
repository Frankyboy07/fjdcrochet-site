Textured Book Sleeve & Cable Pouch

Project Journal | August 2026

Project Overview: Working through a textured pouch design using a warm mustard-gold worsted yarn. Featuring side cable details, single/double crochet ribbing, and a dragon scale stitch flap closure designed to fit a Kindle or small notebook.

The construction started from the center foundation loop and expanded into structured post-stitch cabling along the side edges. Marker placement was critical to align the scale stitches for the main fold-over flap.

Project Progression

Starting the foundation loop with a 4.0mm hook.
Working the front-post cable borders and layered scale stitch section.
Sizing check against the device cover before finishing side seams.
Full piece laid out flat to verify length and flap symmetry.
Pinning side seams with locking stitch markers prior to joining.
Completed textured pouch featuring cable trim and scale flap closure.
